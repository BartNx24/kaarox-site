Prolonged Share

Your business card can keep moving after the first share

Prolonged Share is designed to help a card reach more people who may be interested in the business, product, or service. Its availability depends on the selected plan, sharing settings, limits, and availability period.

✓ Start by sharing your digital business card with a recipient.
✓ Eligible recipients may help extend the card’s visibility to additional relevant users.
✓ You remain subject to your selected sharing preferences, limits, and availability period.

FAQ

Answers to common questions

What is Prolonged Share?

It is a visibility feature that can allow a shared digital card to continue reaching additional relevant users, depending on the selected settings, limits, availability period, and plan.

How can people discover my business?

Users can search for service providers by industry and location. Profiles, cards, posts, and eligible promoted visibility features may also help users find relevant businesses.

Does Kaarox process marketplace payments?

No. Marketplace payments, delivery, returns, and other transaction arrangements are handled directly between buyers and sellers outside Kaarox.

Do I need an account?

Certain features require an account. Other features may also require an internet connection, device permissions, or an active membership.

Can I control who contacts me?

Kaarox includes privacy, reporting, and blocking tools. The exact controls available may depend on the feature being used.
