This Privacy Policy explains how Kaarox (“Kaarox”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Kaarox mobile application, website, and related services.

This Privacy Policy applies to users, businesses, professionals, creators, service providers, buyers, sellers, and anyone else who uses Kaarox.

1. Age Requirement

Kaarox is intended only for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. You must be at least 18 years old to create or maintain a Kaarox account.

During registration, we collect your date of birth to determine whether you meet Kaarox's minimum age requirement.

If we learn that an account belongs to, or is being used by, a person under the age of 18, we may restrict, suspend, or delete the account and associated information as appropriate.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Kaarox, we may collect the following categories of information.

  • Account information, such as your name, username, email address, authentication information, account identifiers, and account status
  • Your date of birth, which is used to determine whether you meet Kaarox's minimum age requirement
  • Profile, personal page, professional profile, and digital business card information you choose to provide
  • Contact information you choose to make available, such as phone number, email address, website, social links, or business address
  • Industry, profession, services, skills, business details, descriptions, slogans, profile photographs, and other profile information
  • Posts, comments, likes, ratings, reviews, groups, hubs, events, bookings, and other content you create or interact with
  • Messages, enquiries, and other communications sent through Kaarox
  • Marketplace or selling information, including listings, item descriptions, prices, photographs, availability, seller information, buyer enquiries, reports, and related communications
  • Card-sharing information, including information about cards you share, receive, open, interact with, or make available for sharing
  • Information relating to sharing and discovery features, including industry preferences, visibility settings, and other settings used to determine which cards, profiles, services, or content may be relevant to you
  • Usage information, such as app interactions, screens viewed, features used, searches, clicks, card views, activity logs, and other app activity
  • Device and technical information, such as device type, operating system, app version, identifiers, network information, crash information, diagnostic information, and performance information
  • Location information if you enable location-based features or grant Kaarox permission to access your device location
  • Subscription, plan, trial, purchase, and payment-status information for Kaarox paid features
  • Reports, complaints, moderation information, fraud-prevention information, safety information, and information relating to violations of our policies
  • Communications you send to Kaarox, including customer-support requests, privacy requests, complaints, feedback, and other enquiries

3. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Create, authenticate, verify, and manage Kaarox accounts
  • Confirm that users meet Kaarox's minimum age requirement
  • Operate Kaarox features, including digital business cards, personal pages, professional profiles, messaging, subscriptions, bookings, posts, groups, hubs, marketplace listings, and card sharing
  • Allow users to discover businesses, professionals, service providers, cards, profiles, posts, groups, listings, and other relevant content
  • Personalise or rank content based on information such as selected industry, location, activity, preferences, and app settings
  • Support location-based discovery and other location-enabled features
  • Allow users to communicate about services, bookings, listings, business opportunities, or other Kaarox activity
  • Deliver and manage messages and notifications
  • Manage subscriptions, trials, account access, and paid Kaarox features
  • Measure card views, content interactions, and other app analytics
  • Diagnose technical problems, monitor performance, improve Kaarox, and develop new functionality
  • Detect scams, prohibited content, fake accounts, abuse, spam, fraud, security threats, and misuse of Kaarox
  • Investigate reports, complaints, disputes, and potential violations of our Terms of Service or Community Guidelines
  • Protect the rights, safety, property, and security of Kaarox, our users, and others
  • Send account notices, verification emails, security messages, service communications, and important updates
  • Send promotional or marketing communications where you have opted in or where otherwise permitted by applicable law
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, tax, safety, enforcement, and governmental requirements

4. Profiles, Cards, Public Information, and Sharing

Kaarox is designed to help users and businesses discover and communicate with one another. As a result, some information you provide may be visible to other users depending on the feature you use and your privacy or visibility settings.

Information that may be visible to other users can include your profile, username, profile photograph, professional information, digital business card, personal page, business information, services, industry, website, public contact information, posts, comments, groups, hubs, marketplace listings, and other information you choose to publish or share.

When you share a digital business card or make information available through Kaarox's sharing features, other users may receive, view, interact with, save, or share that information depending on the feature, your settings, and the applicable sharing rules.

Certain Kaarox features may allow a card that has been shared to be shared again with other relevant users when applicable sharing, industry, visibility, location, or feature conditions are met.

You should only make information public that you are comfortable sharing with other users.

5. Location Data

If you enable location-based features or grant location permission, Kaarox may collect and use precise or approximate location information to support features such as nearby discovery, card sharing, city-based visibility, service discovery, marketplace listings, and other location-based functionality.

Location information may also be used to improve the relevance of results, recommendations, and features that depend on geographic proximity.

You can control location permissions through your device settings. Some Kaarox functionality may not work correctly or may be less useful if location access is disabled.

6. Messages and Communications

Kaarox may process messages and communication-related information in order to deliver messages, maintain conversations, enable user interactions, investigate reports, prevent abuse, protect users, enforce our policies, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Users should not send passwords, verification codes, financial credentials, identity documents, highly sensitive personal information, or other private information unless they are certain that doing so is necessary and appropriate.

Kaarox currently provides text-based messaging functionality. Marketplace buyers and sellers may also use messaging to communicate about listings, services, collection, delivery, or other arrangements.

7. Marketplace and Selling Features

Kaarox may allow users to list, promote, advertise, buy, sell, or exchange items through marketplace-style features.

When you use these features, we may collect and process information relating to your listings, enquiries, communications, photographs, item information, reports, activity, and interactions with other users.

Kaarox does not currently provide direct in-app payment processing for marketplace transactions between buyers and sellers.

Kaarox therefore does not collect, hold, process, refund, guarantee, or manage payments made between users for marketplace transactions.

Buyers and sellers are responsible for agreeing between themselves on payment methods, delivery, collection, refunds, returns, warranties, applicable taxes, and other transaction terms.

Users should exercise caution when sharing personal information, arranging payments, meeting another user, organising collection or delivery, or completing a transaction outside Kaarox.

We may use marketplace-related information to operate marketplace features, display listings, support communication, investigate reports, detect fraud or scams, enforce our policies, protect users, and comply with legal obligations.

8. Payments and Subscriptions

Kaarox may offer paid plans, subscriptions, free trials, promotional visibility, or other paid features.

Where payment is required for Kaarox services, payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers, Apple App Store, Google Play, or other authorised payment processors.

Kaarox may receive information relating to the transaction, such as subscription status, plan type, purchase status, transaction identifiers, renewal status, or entitlement information.

Kaarox does not currently process payments between buyers and sellers for user-to-user marketplace transactions inside the app.

9. Service Providers and Sharing of Information

We may share or make personal information available to service providers that perform services on behalf of Kaarox.

These providers may include companies that provide:

  • Cloud hosting and database services
  • Authentication and account-management services
  • Email verification and communications
  • Push notifications
  • Analytics and performance monitoring
  • Crash reporting and technical diagnostics
  • Security, anti-fraud, and abuse-prevention services
  • Moderation and customer-support services
  • Subscription and payment-processing services
  • Other technology necessary to operate Kaarox

Service providers are permitted to process information only for the services they provide to us, subject to applicable agreements and legal requirements.

We may also disclose personal information where reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law, regulation, court order, or legal process
  • Respond to valid requests from governmental or law-enforcement authorities
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, or illegal activity
  • Protect users or other persons from harm
  • Enforce our Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, or other policies
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal rights or claims
  • Complete a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, subject to applicable privacy requirements

10. Sale and Advertising Use of Personal Information

Kaarox does not currently sell personal information to third parties for monetary consideration.

If Kaarox introduces advertising, targeted advertising, or other data uses that require additional disclosures or choices under applicable privacy laws, this Privacy Policy and the relevant user controls will be updated before or when those practices are introduced.

11. International Data Processing and Transfers

Kaarox operates internationally. Depending on where you use Kaarox and the service providers we use, your personal information may be stored, processed, transferred, or accessed in countries other than the country where you live.

These countries may have privacy and data-protection laws that differ from those in your country.

We may use international service providers for services such as hosting, cloud databases, authentication, analytics, notifications, security, customer support, and subscription processing.

Where required by applicable law, Kaarox takes reasonable steps to ensure that international transfers or disclosures of personal information receive appropriate privacy and security protections.

12. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These rights may include the ability to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of certain personal information
  • Request a copy or export of certain personal information
  • Withdraw certain consents where processing is based on consent
  • Manage marketing preferences
  • Manage certain privacy and visibility settings
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable
  • Submit a privacy complaint

You can manage certain permissions and consents through: Settings → Privacy & Consents, where available.

You can also control operating-system permissions, including location and notification permissions, through your device settings.

To exercise a privacy right that cannot be completed directly through the app, contact us using the information provided in the Contact section below.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain privacy requests.

Some information may need to be retained where permitted or required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, safety, dispute resolution, accounting, security, enforcement of our agreements, or other legitimate purposes.

13. United States Privacy Rights

Users in the United States may have additional privacy rights under applicable federal or state law.

Depending on the state in which you live and whether the applicable legal requirements apply to Kaarox, those rights may include the right to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of personal information and, where applicable, the ability to opt out of certain sales, targeted advertising, or profiling activities.

Kaarox does not currently sell personal information for monetary consideration.

Kaarox will not discriminate against a user for exercising a privacy right where such discrimination is prohibited by applicable law.

Privacy requests can be submitted using the contact information provided below.

14. New Zealand Privacy Rights

If the New Zealand Privacy Act applies to your use of Kaarox, you may have rights relating to personal information held about you, including rights to request access to and correction of your personal information.

Kaarox has designated a Privacy Officer responsible for supporting privacy compliance and responding to privacy requests and concerns.

New Zealand users can contact the Kaarox Privacy Officer using the contact information provided below.

Where required by applicable New Zealand privacy law, Kaarox will take appropriate steps when personal information is disclosed internationally and will respond to qualifying privacy breaches in accordance with applicable notification requirements.

15. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Retention periods may depend on the type of information, how it is used, whether an account remains active, safety and fraud-prevention requirements, contractual requirements, dispute-resolution needs, and applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

For example, certain account information may be retained while your account remains active, while some security, fraud-prevention, transaction, reporting, or legal records may be retained after an account is deleted where reasonably necessary or legally required.

Your date of birth may be retained as part of your account information for purposes including confirming and maintaining compliance with Kaarox's minimum age requirement.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete, anonymise, or otherwise securely dispose of it, subject to applicable law.

16. Security

Kaarox applies reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational security measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

These measures may include account authentication, email verification, access controls, security monitoring, technical safeguards, and protections provided by our infrastructure and service providers.

However, no mobile application, website, database, transmission method, or online service can guarantee absolute security.

If we become aware of a data breach, we will assess the incident and make notifications to users or regulatory authorities where required by applicable law.

17. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Kaarox account and associated personal information using available account-management features or by contacting us.

Account deletion may not result in the immediate deletion of every record. We may retain certain information where reasonably necessary or permitted for legal compliance, safety, fraud prevention, security, dispute handling, accounting, enforcement, or other legitimate purposes.

Information that you shared with other users before deleting your account may also remain available to those users where it was independently saved, shared, or received by them.

18. Children and Minors

Kaarox is an 18+ service. Individuals under the age of 18 are not permitted to create or maintain a Kaarox account.

Kaarox is not intended to be used to exploit, groom, endanger, abuse, or improperly target minors.

Users must not use Kaarox to solicit inappropriate content from minors, request sensitive or private information from minors, facilitate sexual exploitation, or otherwise engage in conduct that puts children at risk.

If we become aware that an individual under 18 has created or is using a Kaarox account, we may suspend or delete the account and take other appropriate action.

19. Reports, Safety, and Legal Compliance

Kaarox provides reporting and moderation mechanisms intended to help users report prohibited, abusive, unsafe, fraudulent, or potentially illegal activity or content.

We may collect and process information associated with reports, including the reported content, relevant communications, account information, supporting information provided by users, moderation decisions, and related safety records.

Kaarox may preserve, restrict, remove, or disclose information where reasonably necessary to investigate reports, protect users, comply with applicable laws, respond to valid legal requests, or enforce Kaarox policies.

20. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Kaarox, our practices, technology, legal requirements, or the countries where Kaarox operates.

The latest version will be published on this page together with the date of the most recent update.

If we make material changes, we may provide additional notice through the Kaarox app, website, email, or another appropriate method.
